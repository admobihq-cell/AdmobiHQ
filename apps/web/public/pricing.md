## Taxi-top LED: spot/play formula

```
total = base_price_per_play × slot_multiplier × zone_multiplier × volume_multiplier × plays_per_day × screens × days
```

- **Base price per play:** KES 8, at the 10s reference slot, per screen
- Multipliers are illustrative and confirmed per brief; loop capacity (max sellable plays/day per screen) is confirmed at booking.

### Slot length multiplier (sub-linear)

| Slot length | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| 10s | 1.0x |
| 15s | 1.3x |
| 30s | 1.8x |
| 60s | 2.5x |

### Zone multiplier

| Zone tier | Multiplier | Example Nairobi areas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community zones | 1.0x | Kibera, Kayole, Dandora, Umoja, Eastlands |
| Premium estates | 1.5x | Kilimani, Lavington, Westlands, Kileleshwa, Hurlingham |
| Elite corridors | 2.0x | CBD, Karen, Gigiri, Airport Road / JKIA corridor |
| All screens (flat, no zone picked) | 1.6x | Every zone above, network-wide |

### Volume multiplier

| Screens booked | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| 1–9 screens | 1.0x |
| 10–49 screens | 0.9x |
| 50+ screens | 0.8x |

### Worked example

1 screen, Premium zone (Kilimani), 15s slot, 20 plays/day, 14 days:
8 × 1.3 × 1.5 × 1.0 × 20 plays × 1 screen × 14 days = **KES 4,368 total**

## Ways to book

### Zone select

- **Tagline:** Pick your corridors
- **Price:** From KES 8 / play at Community rate
- Choose from Community, Premium, and Elite multipliers
- Mix zones across a single campaign
- Best for launches with a defined target audience or neighbourhood

### All screens

- **Tagline:** One flat citywide rate
- **Price:** Flat 1.6x multiplier, any screen
- Flat 1.6x rate regardless of corridor
- Simplest setup for city-wide brand pushes
- No per-zone accounting on your invoice

- **Price:** Quoted on brief
- Corridor or category exclusivity
- 50+ screen fleet commitments with dedicated pacing
